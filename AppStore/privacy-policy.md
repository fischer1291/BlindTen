# Blind Ten Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Blind Ten does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We do not run any servers for the app.

## What stays on your phone

Player names, emoji avatars, groups, game results, statistics and house-rule cards are stored only on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases

The optional Party Pack is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the Party Pack is unlocked on your account.

## Tracking and analytics

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## Children

The app contains no accounts and collects no data, so it collects no data from children either.
